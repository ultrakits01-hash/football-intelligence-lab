# FIL v3.0.0 — Prospect Intelligence

- Wonderkids becomes a scouting workspace rather than a catalogue grid.
- Click any worldwide prospect to open a Prospect Intelligence dossier.
- Adds development-pathway evidence, age-cohort context, discovery stages, value rank and analytics availability.
- Adds local saved-prospect watchlist state.
- Adds discovery lenses: breakthrough, first-team, emerging value and unpriced discoveries.
- Adds sorting by sourced market value, evidence coverage, senior minutes or youngest first.
- Global-only prospects remain usable dossiers instead of dead Player Lab links.
- FIL-linked prospects retain direct Player Lab navigation.
- Evidence and market-value context are explicitly not potential ratings.
- Missing academy/youth data remains missing rather than being represented as zero.
