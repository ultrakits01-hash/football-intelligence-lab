# FIL v2.8.0 — Recruitment Realism + Wonderkids

## Transfer Lab
- Budget ceiling with automatic club-market-level default and manual overrides.
- Club-level realism gate prevents obviously implausible high-value candidates dominating smaller-club searches.
- Profile Match replaces saturated Profile Fit: role-specific, top-end compressed and capped at 92%.
- Position-specific role libraries for GK, DEF, MID and FWD.
- Goalkeepers are now selectable in Transfer Lab.
- Missing metrics are excluded from role scoring rather than treated as zero.

## Wonderkids
- New left-navigation Wonderkids workspace.
- Scans all installed 2025/26+ FIL datasets for players aged 14–19 and deduplicates provider aliases.
- Up to 500 results with age, club, competition, position, nationality, senior minutes and sourced market value where available.
- Filters: max age, position, senior minutes, maximum value and text search.
- Evidence Index measures data evidence only; it is explicitly not a potential rating.
- Player cards route directly into Player Lab.

## Research direction
The global youth board deliberately starts from verifiable installed player data. Guardian Next Generation 2024/2025 research is useful for future academy-only seed expansion, but FIL does not fabricate youth goals, caps or values when a reliable structured source is absent.
