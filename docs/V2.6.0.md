# FIL v2.6.0 — Position-aware Player Lab

Player Lab now changes emphasis by role instead of forcing attacking visuals on every player.

- DEF: defensive/build-up metrics first; shooting only appears with a meaningful sample.
- MID: involvement, passing/carrying and creation first; shooting is secondary unless the sample warrants it.
- FWD: attacking KPIs and shot visuals remain prominent.
- GK: keeper-specific coverage state; no fake shot-based goalkeeper analysis.
- Event XY, when genuinely available, remains the preferred spatial view for outfield roles.
- Shot-only providers are explicitly labelled and never presented as touch heatmaps.
- Position-aware hero KPIs and metric ordering.

No new black-box rating is introduced and no missing defensive/goalkeeping data is inferred.
