# FIL v2.9.0 — Worldwide Wonderkids

- Wonderkids is no longer limited to installed Big Five datasets.
- New one-time global youth importer builds an age 14–19 worldwide catalogue from the Transfermarkt-derived public research dataset already used by FIL's market-value layer.
- Global records carry club, nationality, position, DOB/age and sourced historical market value where available.
- Installed FIL analytics are identity-merged onto global prospects when a safe player match exists.
- Up to 500 filtered prospects are returned at once; the underlying catalogue can be much larger.
- Source filter: Worldwide + FIL / Worldwide catalogue / FIL analytics only.
- Global-only prospects remain useful discovery records instead of disappearing because FIL lacks match-event data.
- Evidence Index remains a data-coverage indicator, never a potential rating.
- No academy goals, youth caps or youth goals are invented. Those require a separate sourced youth-performance layer.

Research direction: Guardian Next Generation provides annual globally curated cohorts (2024: born 2007; 2025: born 2008), GOAL NXGN 2026 covers 50 players born 2007+, and public Transfermarkt-derived data provides broad identity/value coverage. FIL keeps editorial selection signals separate from statistical evidence.
